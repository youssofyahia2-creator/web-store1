Shipping Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

1) Delivery Coverage

We ship within Egypt, and the most suitable shipping company is chosen based on the governorate.

2) Processing and Shipping Time

  • Order processing: 1 to 3 business days.
  • Shipping: 2 to 5 business days depending on the area.

3) Shipping Fees

Shipping fees are shown during order confirmation based on the address and governorate.

4) Unexpected Delays

Delays may occur due to shipping congestion or force majeure, and the customer is notified immediately of any change.

5) Receiving Your Order

Please make sure your address and phone number are correct to avoid delivery delays.
