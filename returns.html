Exchange & Returns Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

1) Allowed Timeframe

Exchange or return requests can be made within 7 days of the delivery date.

2) Exchange/Return Conditions

  • The product must be in its original condition and unused.
  • Original packaging should be included where possible.
  • No damage caused by misuse.

3) Custom Products

Orders custom-designed for the customer cannot be returned unless there is a clear manufacturing defect.

4) Shipping Fees

The customer may bear the exchange/return shipping fees depending on the reason for the request and the carrier's policy.

5) How to Request

Requests are submitted by contacting us directly on WhatsApp with the order number and the reason for the exchange or return.
